The Process

Eight steps, in your hands

Select any step to see what happens there — and why it matters.

Farm & MandiPhoto slot

Raw material, chosen at source

Our buyers work directly with farming families and mandis across Saurashtra, selecting lots by grain size, colour and maturity — before price is even discussed.

  • Direct farm relationships, season after season
  • Sampling before acceptance — failed lots are returned whole
  • Groundnut, pulses and spices from their best-known growing belts
Cleaning LinePhoto slot

Cleaning, without chemicals

Multi-stage machine cleaning removes dust, stones, husk and field debris using sieves, air and gravity — never washes or chemical treatments.

  • De-stoners and aspiration channels
  • Magnetic separation for metal safety
  • No polishing agents at any stage
Grading & SortingPhoto slot

Sorting & grading

Grains are graded by size and density so every pack cooks evenly. Off-size and immature grains are separated out — and never re-blended in.

  • Sieve grading to uniform size bands
  • Gravity separation for density
  • Final visual check by trained staff
Ghani & MillPhoto slot

Processing, the slow way

Oil is pressed on wooden ghanis at low temperature; ghee is bilona-made from A2 Gir cow milk; dals are milled gently to protect the grain.

  • Kachi ghani cold pressing — no refining, no solvents
  • Traditional bilona churn for ghee
  • Low-heat milling that preserves nutrition
Packing LinePhoto slot

Packaging that protects

Products move straight from the line into food-grade, aroma-sealed packs — filled, weighed and sealed in a hygienic packing room.

  • Food-grade films, jars and bottles
  • Checked net weights on every unit
  • Batch and date coding printed at fill time
Quality BenchPhoto slot

Quality check, twice over

Finished packs are re-tested against the same specification as the raw material — moisture, purity, seal integrity and label accuracy.

  • Batch release only after full clearance
  • Retention sample stored from every batch
  • Seal and drop tests on packed units
WarehousePhoto slot

Storage, first in first out

Finished goods rest in a cool, dry, pest-controlled store on pallets — rotated strictly first-in-first-out so nothing waits long.

  • Temperature and pest monitoring
  • Palletised, off-floor storage
  • FIFO rotation enforced by batch code
Dispatch BayPhoto slot

Dispatch, documented

Orders leave with batch records, invoices and — for export — full documentation. Fresh stock reaches retailers, hotels and international buyers.

  • Order-wise batch mapping
  • Export documentation on request
  • Careful cartoning for transit
