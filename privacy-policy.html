MK Enterprise ("we", "us"), the maker of SAURAM products, respects the trust you place in us — on the plate and on this website. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have.

1. What we collect

  • Information you give us — name, email, phone, company and message when you send an inquiry, apply as a distributor, apply for a job, or subscribe to our newsletter.
  • Order information — delivery address and order details when you buy from us directly.
  • Usage information — standard analytics data such as pages viewed and approximate location, if analytics is enabled on this site.

2. How we use it

  1. To respond to inquiries and fulfil orders.
  2. To evaluate distributor, export and career applications.
  3. To send the newsletter you asked for — never more than we promised, and you can leave any time.
  4. To improve the website and our products.

We do not sell your personal information. Ever.

3. When we share

Only with service providers who help us operate — for example courier partners for deliveries and an email provider for the newsletter — and only what they need. We may disclose information where the law requires it.

4. Cookies & local storage

This site uses browser local storage for small conveniences — your wishlist, compare list and recent searches stay on your device and are never transmitted to us. Analytics cookies, if enabled, are described in the cookie notice at first visit.

5. Your rights

You may request a copy, correction or deletion of the personal information we hold about you, and unsubscribe from marketing at any time. Write to us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the data we handle. No internet transmission is perfectly secure; we keep the data we hold to the minimum we need.
