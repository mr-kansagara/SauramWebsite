Shipping Policy

How SAURAM packs travel — timelines, packaging, tracking and international consignments.

Delivery timelines Transit packaging Tracking Charges International

1. Delivery timelines

Orders dispatch from our facility at Bhayavadar, Rajkot district, Gujarat. Typical timelines after dispatch (indicative placeholders — confirm with your logistics partners):

Orders confirmed before noon usually dispatch the same or next business day, subject to batch availability — freshness beats speed when the two conflict.

2. Transit packaging

  • Bottles and jars ship upright in partitioned double-wall cartons.
  • Pouches ship in lined boxes with void fill — no crushed corners, no split seams.
  • Every carton is batch-mapped, so a transit issue can be traced and replaced fast.

3. Tracking

You receive the courier name and tracking number by message or email at dispatch. Trade orders receive a consolidated dispatch note with carton-wise contents.

4. Shipping charges

Charges, where applicable, are shown on your quotation or order confirmation before you commit — no surprises at the door. Free-delivery thresholds for retail orders are announced on the website when active.

5. International shipping

International orders move as export consignments — by sea for container loads, by air for samples and urgent trade orders — with the full document set (invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, phytosanitary certificate where applicable). Start at the export page; incoterms, insurance and lead times are agreed per order.

One promise If a delay ever puts a batch's freshness in question, we hold the dispatch and tell you — we would rather ship late than ship less than our best.
