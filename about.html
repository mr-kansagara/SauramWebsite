Rooted in purity.

SAURAM is the food brand of MK Enterprise — a Saurashtra manufacturer that believes the old ways of making food were right, and modern quality discipline makes them better.

Who We Are

A manufacturer,
not a middleman

At SAURAM, every meal begins with pure, quality ingredients. We bring together traditional values and modern quality standards to deliver premium pulses, spices, cold-pressed oils and dairy you can trust.

We work directly with farming families across Saurashtra, process everything in our own facility, and put our name on every pack. No blending houses, no re-badging, no shortcuts — every product reflects our promise of purity, authenticity and excellence.
