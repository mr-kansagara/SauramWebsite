Our Story

Born in Saurashtra,
the Land of the Sun

“Purity is not a marketing word in our house. It is how our family has always eaten.”

SAURAM began with a simple frustration: food that looked premium but wasn't pure. So MK Enterprise went back to the source — the sun-fed fields of Saurashtra — and built a facility where traditional methods meet modern quality control.

Every product reflects our promise of purity, authenticity and excellence. Our mission is to put honest, traditional food within reach of every family. Our vision is a SAURAM pack in every kitchen that refuses to compromise.
